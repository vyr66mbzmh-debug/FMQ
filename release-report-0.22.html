Luisa v0.22 report

Luisa v0.22 is published. Mechanics Year 1 is complete: 114 slides and four student booklets totalling 58 A4 pages, within the Statistics and Mechanics Year 1/AS textbook.

  • Mechanics Year 1 Chapter 8 — Modelling in mechanics: 9 slides; 5 booklet pages.
  • Mechanics Year 1 Chapter 9 — Constant acceleration: 42 slides; 21 booklet pages.
  • Mechanics Year 1 Chapter 10 — Forces and motion: 41 slides; 21 booklet pages.
  • Mechanics Year 1 Chapter 11 — Variable acceleration: 22 slides; 11 booklet pages.

All 114 slides and all 58 booklet pages were visually reviewed. Three screen sizes, answer concealment, click/Next/Reset and Space reveal order passed. Booklets have two slides per page with expanded writing areas. User acceptance is separate from these checks.

Master folder and recovery

The master is now Programming/_LUISA: website/Git working tree, original sources, editable LaTeX and lesson recipes, questions/solutions, current booklets, verification records, local launcher and latest website recovery ZIP. The FMQ folders within _GPT have been retired after verification. Unpublished older work has been retained as source material. Credentials are excluded.

The latest site has also been copied to ADA /workspace/public. The existing GitHub repository and Cloudflare URL are retained.

Remaining work and usage

Mechanics Year 2 chapters 4 (Moments), 6 (Projectiles), 7 (Applications of forces) and 8 (Further kinematics) remain. Chapter 5 was already checked. New chapter work stopped at 22% remaining to reserve migration/publication allowance; the snapshot before publication was 20% remaining. Usage is account-wide, not an exact task measurement.
