# FMQ lesson conversion standard — V11 and all future courses

Applies to all 14 Pure Year 1 chapters and future Pure Year 2, Mechanics, Statistics and Further Mathematics. User feedback of 24 October 2026 supersedes the V10 semantic redesign.

## Content fidelity
- Original PowerPoint is the authority for teaching content, ordering, diagram relationships, individual blue covers and animation groups. Re-read updated sources (including Chapter 8 slide 17).
- Preserve every explanation, definition, example, hint, reflection, formula-booklet notation and meaningful arrow. No summarising away steps, introducing unfamiliar notation such as T_(r+1), or replacing the full binomial formula by summation notation.
- Correct actual errors using the textbook, solution bank and mathematical verification. Keep correction records private; no source-discrepancy notes, alternate erroneous working or editorial commentary in lessons/booklets.
- Black source panels remain distinct boxed theory/hint panels with contrasting backgrounds and their source relationships intact.

## Animation and answers
- Reconstruct PowerPoint main click sequence and independent trigger sequences from source timing, including with/after-previous grouping, paragraph ranges, arrows, motion, entrances and exits. Preserve four teaching states for Chapter 8 slide 14 and right-to-left arrow on slide 16.
- Theory can be animated but is supplied student material. Only original concealed question answers/working are blue after reveal and omitted in student print.
- One original answer cover means one independent reveal. Preserve parts a,b,c, questions 5,6,7 and other separate covers; never merge them.
- Use ? while hidden and a tick when revealed; remove Reveal working/reasoning/Working 1 labels. Touch-accessible Next advances the original teaching sequence. Disabled state communicates completion; no teaching-reveals counter.
- Keep Pascal triangles centred. Binomial coefficient entries must be centred in their parentheses.

## Layout
- Preserve original question–answer grouping and layout, including vertical question/answer/next-question sequences and tables. Keep each complete prompt with its answer.
- Consistently indent numbered/bulleted lists and nested lettered parts, correct automatic-list counters without changing genuine textbook numbers.
- Scale the slide to available board/laptop screen; use generous legible type, centred larger title in a blue banner. Title pages: blue background/yellow text. Extension/STEP/MAT/AEA titles: red.
- Overview is a large contents list. Preserve original full content and notation while improving alignment/spacing.
- No repeated footer course/chapter/original-slide boilerplate. Display an individual slide number matching the booklet.

## Student lesson booklet
- Separate from existing Print Skill / Print Topic question printouts, which remain exactly as originally designed.
- One complete-chapter theory booklet matching the board. Remove lesson-booklet skill/topic variants from the UI.
- Two slides per A4 page. Each slide has its own blue banner with exactly the board title and slide number; no pair/page numbering replacing slide numbers, no skill-code heading.
- Include all non-answer content regardless of animation: theory, examples, instructions, definitions, hints, diagrams and arrows.
- Replace original blue-covered answer regions with appropriately sized blank writing space. Do not omit anything else by generic heuristics. Preserve separate parts and relative writing-space sizes.
- Use full available width for question prose where original layout permits, reducing wrapping to maximise writing room.

## Navigation and release
- Teaching slides and book links open complete chapter; selected skill determines chapter, multi-chapter chooser where needed. 1.01/1.02 -> Chapter 1; 1.29 -> Chapter 8.
- FMQ library returns to site root preserving selection.
- Increment public version once per developed release, maintain changelog against previous release and recoverable Git tag plus local release source snapshot. V10 preserved at 7b19511209c0c277a0f169e0db53ca9af4cfcb23; this release is V11.

## Acceptance
- Source/output comparison for all slides, all source click/trigger groups accounted for, mathematical compilation and layout checks, board/student content parity and complete booklet page review.
- Never treat a successful automated rendering check as proof of source fidelity.
